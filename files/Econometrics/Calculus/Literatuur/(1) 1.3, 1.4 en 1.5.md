### 1.3: New Functions from Old Functions
---
Lets first consider **translations** of graphs:
- If $c$ is a positive number, then the graph of $y = f(x) + c$ is just the graph of $y = f(x)$ shifted upward a distance of $c$ units.
- If $g(x) = f(x - c)$ where $c > 0$ then the value of $g$ at $x$ is the same value of $f$ at $x - c$. The graph is shifted $c$ units to the right

**Vertical and Horizontal Shifts:** Suppose $c > 0$. To obtain the graph of
- $y = f(x) + c$, shift $y = f(x)$ a distance $c$ upwards.
- $y = f(x) - c$, shift $y = f(x)$ a distance $c$ downwards
- $y = f(x - c)$, shift $y = f(x)$ a distance $c$ to the right
- $y = f(x + c)$, shift $y = f(x)$ a distance $c$ to the left

Lets consider **stretching** and **reflecting** transformations. (Suppose $c > 1$)
- $y = c f(x)$ stretch the graph of $y = f(x)$ vertically by a factor $c$
- $y = (1/c) f(x)$ shrink the graph of $y = f(x)$ vertically by a factor $c$
